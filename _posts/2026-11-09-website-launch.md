This website presents WorKnow’s research, its team, and its publications, events and other activities. News, calls for papers and workshop programmes will be posted here as the project develops.

The research team comprises the Principal Investigator, Raffaele Danna, and five postdoctoral researchers: Giuliana Freschi, Alessandro Brioschi, Andrea Papi, Jip Van Besouw and Francesca Masiero. You can read more about them on the [Team page]({{ '/team/' | relative_url }}).
