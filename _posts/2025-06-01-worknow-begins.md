title: WorKnow begins
---
WorKnow – *Working Knowledge: The Emergence of Practitioners in Late Medieval and Early Modern Europe* – started in June 2025 at the Sant’Anna School of Advanced Studies in Pisa.

The project is funded for three years by a Starting Grant from the Fondo Italiano per la Scienza (grant FIS 2023-01508), with Raffaele Danna as Principal Investigator. It brings together the history of science and economic history to study how practitioners and practical knowledge emerged in Europe from the late Middle Ages onwards.
